Authentication flow –
Fast, secure, and user-friendly

I worked with product and security teams to define login and onboarding needs. Usability tests showed long forms discouraged first-time users. I designed a 2-step flow with biometric login support, balancing compliance with ease of use, which improved sign-up completion and built trust early.

Claim request -
Guided, transparent, end-to-end

Through journey mapping and collaboration with stakeholders, I restructured the insurance claim flow to reduce complexity and highlight key steps. The redesigned experience provides users with a clear, trackable process, increasing both confidence and efficiency.

Claim request -
Guided, transparent, end-to-end

An 8-step digital claim submission flow designed for clarity and convenience. Users can submit insurance claims entirely online — from selecting the insured person to uploading documents, verifying OTP, and receiving confirmation — all in a structured, mobile-friendly experience.

Payment top-up –
Flexible, guided, and error-proof

Based on user interviews, I designed a clear, multi-option top-up flow to ensure uninterrupted insurance coverage. Working closely with product owners and developers, I streamlined the process to reduce friction and build user trust.

Payment top-up –
Flexible, guided, and error-proof

A guided premium top-up flow that ensures users pay correctly and confidently. From policy selection to amount input, the interface is designed to reduce errors and enforce business rules. Quick amount shortcuts, real-time feedback, and contextual warnings ensure a smooth and compliant experience.

Fund performance –
Transparent tracking of investment value

Using a combination of user research and data visualization best practices, I transformed complex investment metrics into accessible dashboards. Collaborating with stakeholders, I focused on clarity and transparency, enabling users to track fund growth and make confident decisions.
